Data Analyst Internship

----------------------------------------------------
📌 Project Title:
Superstore Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

📌 Objective:
To design a simple yet interactive dashboard visualizing 
Superstore sales performance across regions, categories, 
months, segments, and payment modes.

📌 Tools Used:
- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Superstore Sales Dataset (CSV)

----------------------------------------------------
📁 Features of the Dashboard:
1. Sales by Region (Donut Chart)
2. Sales by Category (Bar Chart)
3. Sales by Segment (Donut Chart)
4. Sales by Payment Mode (Donut Chart)
5. Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart)
6. Yearly Sales Comparison (Line Chart)
7. Sales by Sub-Category (Bar Chart)
8. KPI Cards (Total Sales, Total Profit, Quantity)
9. Region-wise Sales Map (Filled Map)
10. Slicers for Year and Region

----------------------------------------------------
📊 Key Insights:
1. The Consumer segment contributes the highest share of sales,
   followed by Corporate, while Home Office contributes the least.

2. The West region records the highest overall sales among all regions.

3. Credit Card is the most preferred payment method, generating 
   the maximum share of transactions.

4. Technology is the best-performing product category, outperforming 
   Furniture and Office Supplies.

5. Sales show a noticeable spike between October and December, indicating 
   strong Q4 performance.

6. Phones, Chairs, and Accessories are the top revenue-generating 
   sub-categories.

7. Profitability varies across regions, with some areas showing lower 
   profit margins despite high revenue.
